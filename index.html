How to play

With the ball

Arrows / WASDRun and dribble
ShiftSprint (the ball runs further ahead: easier to lose)
SpacePass to the team-mate in the direction you point (no direction: the way you face)
E (hold)Shoot: hold for power, release to strike. Up / down aims at the far or near side of the goal

Without the ball

SpaceStanding tackle (get close to the ball first)
ESlide tackle in the direction you point. Miss the ball and take the man: a foul
QSwitch to the team-mate best placed for the ball

Rules

TeamFive a side: a keeper and four outfield players. No offside.
RestartsKick-ins from the touchline, corners, goal throws by the keeper
FoulsA free kick; in the penalty area, a penalty
